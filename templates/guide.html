Why Segregation Matters

India generates over 62 million tonnes of municipal solid waste every year, growing at roughly 8% annually. Less than a quarter is scientifically processed — the rest ends up in landfills or open dumps. When recyclables are mixed with wet or hazardous waste, they become contaminated and lose their recycling value.

Source segregation — sorting waste at home before it reaches the collection point — is the single most impactful action a citizen can take. This guide covers the six categories EcoSort AI recognizes.

What Is EcoScore?

Every time you scan a photo, EcoSort AI computes an EcoScore between 0 and 100. It measures how recyclable that batch of waste is, weighted by the model's confidence in each detection. Higher scores mean cleaner, more recyclable batches.

A · 80–100 · Excellent
B · 60–79 · Good
C · 40–59 · Mixed
D · 0–39 · Mostly Trash
