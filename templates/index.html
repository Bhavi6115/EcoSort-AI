How EcoSort AI Works

🎯 Multi-Item Detection

Unlike single-item classifiers, EcoSort detects multiple waste items in a single photo using YOLOv8 trained on the TACO dataset.

🗑️ Smart Disposal Logic

Each detected material maps to a real-world bin color + disposal instruction — bridging the gap between "what is it" and "what to do".

📊 EcoScore Metric

A unique 0–100 score that quantifies how recyclable a scan batch is — enabling tracking, gamification, and insights over time.

🌍 SDG Alignment

Directly supports UN SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities) and SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption) through better waste segregation at the source.
